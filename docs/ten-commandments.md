# The Ten Commandments memory challenge (planned, not built)

Heather's decisions, October 1, 2026:

- **Launch:** March 1, 2027. Family `memory` (Scripture Memory on the hub).
- **Name:** The Ten Commandments (recommended; the hub already files it under
  Scripture Memory, so "memorize" would repeat itself). Confirm before building.
- **Two tracks:** one memorizes the full verses, one memorizes a shortened line
  for each commandment.
- **Translations:** the reader picks NIV, NLT, ESV or KJV, same as Beatitudes.
  So a signup carries two choices: full or short, and a translation.
- **Order:** Protestant, Exodus 20:3-17.
- **Pictures:** Heather's ten number pictures, `images/commandments/cardN.jpg`
  and `cardN-thumb.jpg`. Do not redesign them. 1 dollar bill, 2 kneeling in
  prayer, 3 saying GOD, 4 asleep on a pillow, 5 high-fiving two parents,
  6 gun, 7 kiss, 8 handcuffs, 9 "He did it!", 10 holding a diamond ring.
- **Progression:** cumulative SEE, RECOGNIZE, RECALL, RECITE over 30 days, not
  three days per commandment. Proposed: day 1 all ten pictures and #1; days
  2-10 one new commandment a day with review of the earlier ones; day 11
  catch-up; 12-17 recognize; 18-24 recall with more hidden each day; 25-29
  recite; 30 all ten and the certificate.
- **Do not touch Beatitudes.** Build from a copy of `challenge/js/beatitudes.js`,
  never by editing it, and run the Beatitudes dashboard and emails before and
  after to prove they did not change.
- **Every email editable** in `/admin-emails.html` from day one.
