Built to Shine
For the Woman Leading with
Faith in the Business World
Heather Wilson

You can lead boldly without losing who God created you to be.

Women in leadership often carry expectations that were never theirs to carry.

Be confident, but not intimidating. Be decisive, but always agreeable. Lead boldly, but make sure everyone still likes you. Honor your faith, but keep it separate from your work.

In Built to Shine, Heather Wilson confronts the lies that cause women to question their leadership, minimize their influence, and carry burdens God never asked them to carry.

Through honest stories, practical leadership lessons, and biblical truth, Heather helps women lead with courage, clarity, and faith without losing who God created them to be.

This is not a book about becoming louder, tougher, or more impressive. It is about becoming more faithful to the assignment God has given you.

Built to Shine is for the woman who...

Has questioned whether she is qualified to lead.
Replays difficult conversations and wonders whether she was too much.
Feels pressure to choose between strong leadership and genuine faith.
Has confused being needed with being effective.
Is tired of leading from fear, comparison, control, or the need for approval.
Wants to lead with confidence while remaining grounded in who God created her to be.

Inside Built to Shine

  • How the need to be liked can quietly weaken your leadership.
  • Why control often disguises itself as responsibility.
  • How to recognize burdens God never asked you to carry.
  • What biblical leadership looks like in the business world.
  • How to lead faithfully even when you are misunderstood.
  • How to embrace your influence without apologizing for it.

Meet Heather Wilson

Heather Wilson is the co-founder and co-CEO of GiveSendGo, a global crowdfunding platform created to share the hope of Jesus through generosity.

As a wife, mother of six, entrepreneur, ministry leader, and woman leading in the business world, Heather understands the tension between carrying significant responsibility and staying faithful to who God has called you to be.

Through Built to Shine, she shares lessons learned while building, leading, failing, growing, and discovering that faithful leadership is not about fitting someone else's mold. It is about courageously walking in your own God-given assignment.
