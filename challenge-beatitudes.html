How it works

Each Beatitude comes with what it actually means and what it looks like in real life. You memorize the heart, not just the words.

Your dashboard hides more of the passage each day so you fill it in from memory. Fun, quick, and it actually works.

By Day 30 you can say all of it. Record yourself, share it with #HideItInYourHeart, and get your certificate.

Why the Beatitudes

We have read the Bible wide in July and gone deep in one book in August. This time we are going to carry a piece of it with us everywhere we go.

The Beatitudes are the opening words of the most famous sermon Jesus ever preached. In just a few lines He turns the whole world upside down. The blessed ones are not the rich and powerful. They are the poor in spirit, the mourning, the meek, the merciful.

"Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see God."

I want these words in my heart, not just on my shelf. When my day falls apart, when I do not know what to pray, when I need to remember who I am, I want the Beatitudes right there, already inside me.

That is what this month is. Thirty days of hiding His words in our hearts, one line at a time, until they are ours for good. Come do it with me.
