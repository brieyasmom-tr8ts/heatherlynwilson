One Book Deep: 31 Days in James + Prayer

The challenge starts August 1st

--Days
--Hours
--Min

Every day for 31 days, you will read the entire book of James and pray with a specific focus. Your first email arrives August 1st at 6am Eastern.

What to expect

This is not a race through the Bible. This is depth. You will read one book, James, every single day for a month. Five chapters. About 15 minutes.

Each day comes with a prayer focus tied to James. You will journal what stood out, what you hear God saying, and write your prayer out.

By Day 31, James will be part of you.

Get a head start

Read all of James once before we begin. Five chapters, about 15 minutes. It will make Day 1 land deeper.

Hide It In Your Heart: Memorize the Beatitudes

The challenge starts September 1st

--Days
--Hours
--Min

Every day for 30 days, you will learn a little more of the Beatitudes until you can say the whole thing from memory. Your first email arrives September 1st.

What to expect

We learn the Beatitudes one line at a time. Eight of them over the month, a new one every few days, so it never feels like cramming.

Each day you get a short teaching, a memory game that hides more words as you go, and a practice to lock it in.

By Day 30, the heart of Jesus will be written on yours.

Get ready

A few things to set up before September 1. Tap each one as you do it.

  • Confirm your translationYou picked one at signup. You can change it any time on this dashboard once we start.
  • Set a daily alarmPick the time you will practice. Even five minutes a day is enough to memorize this.
  • Plan where you will post itFridge, mirror, car dashboard. Somewhere you will see the words all day. Printable cards come with the challenge.
  • Invite someoneMemorizing together sticks better. Share the link below with one person.

Around the Table: 31 Days in Proverbs

The challenge starts October 1st

--Days
--Hours
--Min

One chapter of Proverbs a day, as a family. Each morning you get the chapter, the big idea, questions for your kids by age, and one family challenge. Your first email arrives October 1st.

What to expect

Proverbs has 31 chapters and October has 31 days. One chapter a day, ten to fifteen minutes, and the whole book of wisdom by Halloween.

You do not need a quiet house or a perfect dinner table. The car counts. Have a kid read the chapter out loud, or play it on the Bible app while you drive.

Aim for together, not perfect. Missed days do not break anything. Just jump back in.

Get ready

A few things to set up before October 1. Tap each one as you do it.

  • Pick your family's momentDinner table, breakfast, or the car on the school run. Whatever actually happens every day at your house.
  • Decide who readsHave a kid read the verses out loud, take turns, or play the chapter on the Bible app. Readers remember what they read aloud.
  • Set up the Bible appIf you will listen in the car, download the YouVersion app and try the audio once so it is ready on Day 1.
  • Tell the kidsKids do better when they know something is starting. Tell them the family is reading Proverbs together in October.
  • Invite another familyKids love knowing their friends are reading the same chapter. Share the link below.
