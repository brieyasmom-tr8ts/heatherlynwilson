Get ready

Five things to set up before July 1. Tap each one as you do it.

  • Pick your BibleUse whichever translation you read most easily. NLT, NIV, ESV, or the Bible app on your phone all work.
  • Set a daily alarmPick the time you will read. Mornings stick best, but anytime you can show up daily is right.
  • Pick your spotA chair, a corner, a coffee shop. Anywhere you will actually go each day.
  • Grab a journal or highlighterOptional, but writing down or marking what stands out makes a real difference. The Highlighted blog series came from doing this.
  • Read the free guide aboveTwenty minutes now will save you days of frustration during the challenge.
