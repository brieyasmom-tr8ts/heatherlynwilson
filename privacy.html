Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

What this site collects

When you sign up for a challenge, contact me, or subscribe to my emails, I collect your name and email address. If you sign up for a challenge, I also store your reading progress and journal entries so your dashboard works.

I do not sell your information to anyone. I do not share it with anyone. It is used only to run this site and send you the emails you signed up for.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand how people find and use the site. These tools use cookies, which are small files stored in your browser.

I use this data to see things like how many people visited, which pages they looked at, and where traffic came from. I do not use it to identify individuals.

If you do not want to be tracked, you can use your browser's "Do Not Track" setting, use an ad blocker, or browse in incognito mode.

Emails

If you sign up for a challenge or subscribe to my list, you will receive emails from me. Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. If you unsubscribe, I stop emailing you. Emails are sent through Brevo (a transactional email service).

Your data

If you want to know what data I have for you, or if you want me to delete it, email me at heather@heatherlynwilson.com and I will take care of it.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, contact me.
