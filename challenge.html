One thing before you pick

These are not devotionals. I am not handing you three paragraphs of my thoughts with a verse sprinkled on top. Every challenge on this page puts the Bible itself in front of you, big pieces of it, day after day, and lets it do the talking. My morning note just sets the table. The Word is the meal.

"For the word of God is alive and active." Hebrews 4:12
