Read the Bible With Me

Choose a challenge and begin reading the Bible for yourself. No guilt. No perfection required. Just keep showing up.

These are not devotionals. I am not handing you three paragraphs of my thoughts with a verse sprinkled on top. Every challenge on this page puts the Bible itself in front of you, big pieces of it, day after day, and lets it do the talking. My morning note just sets the table. The Word is the meal.

"For the word of God is alive and active." Hebrews 4:12

How it works

Read for yourself. Grow alongside others.

1

Choose a challenge

Pick one that fits where you are. This month's community challenge, a past challenge, or the Bible Reading Challenge on your own schedule.

2

Join for free

Sign up with your name and email. You get a personal dashboard and a daily email from Heather with encouragement and that day's reading.

3

Read from the Bible each day

Open the Bible. Read the actual text. Check in on your dashboard to track your progress and keep your streak going.

4

Create a group and invite friends

Start a group, share an invite link, and read together. See each other's progress, post encouragement, and cheer each other on.

5

Complete the challenge

Finish all the days and receive a certificate and a shareable social graphic to celebrate what you accomplished.

6

Continue into the next one

A new challenge opens every month. Keep the momentum going and let each challenge build on the last.
