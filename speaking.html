Speaking Topics

What I love to talk about.

Each of these can be a keynote, a breakout, or a workshop. I love customizing the message for your audience and your moment.

01

Built to Shine

For women who feel like they're supposed to make themselves smaller to be acceptable. Why you weren't made to shrink, what God says about it, and how to step fully into who He made you to be.

02

Igniting Generosity

Ten years of leading GiveSendGo has taught me what generosity really is. Not the polished version, but the kind that changes both the giver and the receiver. How to live and lead generously, on purpose.

03

Leading Without Losing Yourself

Identity, people-pleasing, and the quiet ways women in leadership slowly disappear inside their own roles. How to lead with strength while staying rooted in who you are.

04

Leading with Faith

What it looks like to lead a real company, raise a real family, and walk through real challenges with your faith fully intact. The pressure, the practice, and what God has taught me along the way.

05

How to Stand Out

For students and young adults. Identity, confidence, and how to be the kind of person who shines in college, in jobs, and in life. Built around the truth that you were wonderfully made.

06

Courage Over Comfort

For women ready to stop hiding. The kind of courage God calls us to is rarely loud. It's the quiet, daily decision to do the next right thing, even when no one is clapping.
