Speaking Topics

What I love to talk about.

Each of these can be a keynote, a breakout, or a workshop. I love customizing the message for your audience and your moment.

01

Courage Over Comfort

The courage God calls us to is rarely loud or glamorous. It is the daily choice to obey Him, speak truth, keep showing up, and do the next right thing even when it feels uncomfortable.

02

The Lies Women Believe

So many women are carrying beliefs that God never said about them. This message unpacks common lies around worth, identity, insecurity, and purpose, and how to replace them with truth.

03

I Am Not a Banana

A fun and meaningful talk about identity, comparison, and learning to stop trying to be who everyone else expects you to be. God did not create you to blend in. He created you on purpose, with purpose.

04

Igniting Generosity

After ten years building GiveSendGo, I have learned that generosity is about far more than money. It is about living open-handed with your time, encouragement, leadership, and life in a way that changes people.

05

Living an Adventure with Jesus

Following Jesus was never meant to be boring or safe. Through stories of business, family, fostering, risks, failures, and faith, this message encourages people to trust God fully and say yes to the adventure He has for them.

06

A Simple Yes

Some of the most meaningful parts of life begin with a simple yes to God. A message about foster care, adoption, parenting, faith, and the unexpected ways God works through ordinary people willing to open their hearts and homes.
