Are You That Dude's Girlfriend?

Learning to Love Like Jesus.

What happens when you say yes to God, and everything gets harder?

When five foster children from hard places showed up at my door, I thought I was ready. I had structure, faith, and a heart to help. What followed was a crash course in chaos, surrender, and the uncomfortable kind of obedience that doesn't come with applause.

This is my raw and redemptive journey through laughter, tears, slammed doors, and moments of whispered prayers in the hallway. With vulnerability, humor, and grit, I invite you to walk with me through the real-life moments of learning to love like Jesus, especially when you feel like there is nothing in it for you. The act of giving really does transform both the giver and the receiver.

I Am NOT a Banana

A children's book about knowing who you are.

What happens when someone calls you something that just isn't true?

Young readers follow Kenzie as she learns an important lesson about identity, words, and confidence. When hurtful names and whispers start to make her question herself, Kenzie's mom helps her see the truth in a simple, memorable way: just because someone says something doesn't make it true.

After all, if someone called you a banana, would that make you one?

Children learn that their identity isn't defined by what others say about them. They are wonderfully made by God, deeply loved, and secure in who they are.
