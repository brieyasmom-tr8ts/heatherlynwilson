I read the entire Bible in January. Now I'm revisiting my highlights and blogging about why each verse stood out.

Psalm 56:4

October 5, 2026
"I praise God for what he has promised. I trust in God, so why should I be afraid? What can mere mortals do to me?"Psalm 56:4 (NLT)

I've always loved this verse.

It reads like the best line in a movie. The kind of thing the hero says right before they walk into the fight. Except this one is better than any movie line, because it isn't make believe.

What can mere mortals do to me?

David isn't being arrogant. He isn't saying nothing bad can ever happen. He isn't pretending the threats aren't real. He is a man who has been chased by kings, betrayed by friends, and hunted for years. He knew what mortals could do.

But he also knew who he was standing behind.

And in comparison to God, the people trying to hurt him were just people. Mortals. Fragile. Temporary. Not on the throne. Not in charge of his soul. Not the ones with the final say.

That is where the peace comes from.

Not from denying the danger. From remembering who is bigger than it.

I want that kind of peace in my own life. The kind that doesn't need everything to feel good before I can worship. The kind that can look at a hard situation and say God, I trust You. What can mere mortals do to me?

Because the answer is honest. They can do a lot. They can hurt me. They can lie about me. They can walk away from me. They can make my life difficult in a thousand ways.

But they can't touch the part of me that belongs to God.

And that is the part that actually matters.

What are you afraid of right now that God might be inviting you to hand over to Him?
