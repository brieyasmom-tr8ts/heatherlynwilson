I read the entire Bible in January. Now I'm revisiting my highlights and blogging about why each verse stood out.

Proverbs 11:24

October 23, 2026
"Give freely and become more wealthy; be stingy and lose everything."Proverbs 11:24 (NLT)

This is one of those upside-down truths that only makes sense in God's economy.

The world tells us to hold on. Keep what is ours. Protect our resources. Look out for ourselves first. And it makes sense on paper. If you give things away, you have less. If you hold on to what you have, you keep more.

But God says the opposite.

Give freely and become more wealthy. Be stingy and lose everything.

I don't think this is just about money. I think it is about a whole way of living. A posture. Because the same principle plays out in almost every area of life.

The person who gives their time freely ends up with a richer life than the one who guards every minute. Open your heart and you'll receive more than the person who keeps it locked down. Serve people generously and you'll be more fulfilled than the one who spends their whole life on themselves. Forgiveness works the same way. Hold every offense and you shrink. Let it go and you walk in more peace than you thought possible.

Generosity multiplies. Stinginess shrinks.

That is just how God designed it.

I've watched this in real life. The most generous people I know are also the most joyful. They have a peace about them, like their lives are full. They give freely, whether it's money or time, encouragement or prayer, or a place at their table. And they never seem to run short because of it. If anything, they have more friends and more laughter, and their relationships are deeper for having given themselves to people.

And I've watched the flip side too. People who hold on tight to everything they have. Their money, their time, their affection, their approval. And they end up smaller for it. Not richer. Just tighter. Losing exactly what they were trying to protect.

Because a life closed off from giving is a life closed off from receiving. You can't open your hand to receive if your fist is closed to give.

God is generous. That's just who He is. He gave His Son, and He keeps giving us grace in ordinary ways too, rain on the just and the unjust, and breath this morning that we didn't earn. When we live generously, we look more like Him. And when we look more like Him, our lives start to bear the kind of fruit only His way can produce.

I want to be that kind of person. I don't want to live all clenched up, figuring out what it will cost me every time before I give. I want to live openhanded, trusting that God will keep filling me back up as I pour out what He has given me.

Where in your life are you being stingy in a way that is actually costing you more than it is protecting you?
