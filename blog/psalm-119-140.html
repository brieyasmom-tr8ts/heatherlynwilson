I read the entire Bible in January. Now I'm revisiting my highlights and blogging about why each verse stood out.

Psalm 119:140

October 14, 2026
"Your promises have been thoroughly tested; that is why I love them so much."Psalm 119:140 (NLT)

Think about who you actually trust in life.

It isn't usually the person who talks the most or makes the biggest promises. It is the one who has come through. Over and over. The one whose word actually holds. The one you know from experience will do what they said they would do.

That is what real trust looks like, and that is exactly what this verse is saying about God.

Your promises have been thoroughly tested.

Not just promised. Tested. Weighed. Held up to the fire and pressure of real situations, real people, real history. God doesn't just say He doesn't lie. He has proven it over thousands of years, through generation after generation, in prison cells and deserts and battlefields and grief and impossible situations and cultures that rose and fell.

His word has stood every time.

If I said I trust someone because they have never failed me, that would mean something. But we are talking about a God whose promises have been proven true for thousands of years by millions of people across every kind of circumstance imaginable. That is a different level of trust. And it is available to us. The promises that carried Abraham, that steadied David, that gave Paul confidence in a prison cell — those are the same ones that carry me today.

There is a lot in this world that promises to be trustworthy. Systems, leaders, plans, formulas, philosophies. Most of them don't survive their first real test. But God's promises have already been through the fire, and they are still standing.

That is a good place to build a life on.

What promise of God are you trusting Him with right now, and does it help to remember it has been tested and proven long before it ever got to you?
