I read the entire Bible in January. Now I'm revisiting my highlights and blogging about why each verse stood out.

Psalm 33:10

September 28, 2026
"The Lord frustrates the plans of the nations and thwarts all their schemes."Psalm 33:10 (NLT)

Sometimes a short verse is exactly what you need.

When I read this it feels like a comfort. Like thank You, God. I needed to hear that.

Because if you look around at the world right now, it is easy to feel like things are spinning out of control. Governments are doing what they want. Leaders are making decisions that affect millions of people. Empires are rising and falling. Wars are being planned. Laws are being passed. Wealth and power are being concentrated in ways that can feel scary.

And you can start to wonder if anyone is actually in charge.

This verse says yes.

God is.

He frustrates the plans of the nations. He thwarts their schemes. Not just one nation. All of them. Not just the ones we don't like. All of them. He is over all of it. And nothing that is being plotted by any human power is going to override His ultimate plan.

That is a big deal.

Because we live in a time where it feels like people with power get to write the script. And when we don't like what they are writing, we feel helpless. Like the story is going in a direction we can't stop.

But we aren't the ones writing the story.

He is.

And no scheme, no plan, no plot, no strategy of any nation on this planet, no matter how big or how organized or how well funded, gets to override what God has already set in motion.

Kingdoms have risen and fallen for thousands of years. Empires that thought they were unstoppable are now footnotes in history books. Rulers who thought they were gods are dust in graves that no one visits anymore. And through all of it, God has kept moving His plan forward.

He isn't intimidated by any of it.

The current leaders aren't the exception. The current crisis isn't the exception. Whatever seems huge and unstoppable right now isn't the exception either. God is still on the throne. He is still working. He is still ultimately in control.

That doesn't mean I sit back and do nothing. It doesn't mean I stop caring about what is happening in the world. It doesn't mean I stop praying or engaging or standing for what is right.

It just means I can stop panicking.

Because panic assumes God isn't in charge. Faith assumes He is.

And when I remember that He frustrates the plans of the nations and thwarts their schemes, I can breathe again. I can do the work in front of me. I can be faithful in my little corner. I can trust that what is being schemed in rooms I'll never sit in is still being watched by the God who has never once lost control of His creation.

That is enough for today.

What has been making you feel like the world is spinning out of control lately, and how does it change things to remember God is still over the plans of every nation?
