Generosity Has to Be Planned

November 16, 2026
"But generous people plan to do what is generous, and they stand firm in their generosity."Isaiah 32:8 (NLT)

Generosity has to be planned.

Otherwise it doesn't really happen. We can talk all day about wanting to be generous, and we can genuinely mean it. But life keeps moving. Money is always pre spent, the budget is always tight, and something always comes up. The generous thing we said we would do never actually happens.

Generous people plan to do what is generous. It is intentional. They set money and time aside, pay attention to who around them needs something, and make room in their lives for the giving before the giving even shows up. Most of us wait to see what is left over, and there is never anything left over. Generous people flip that. They start with the giving and build their lives around it instead of trying to squeeze it in at the end.

The second half of the verse matters too.

They stand firm in their generosity.

Because standing firm implies pressure. And that is exactly what happens when you decide to be generous with what God has given you. There will be second guessing. Voices in your head telling you it doesn't make sense. Fear that you won't have enough for yourself. Generosity often doesn't make sense on paper. When God asks you to give, He is usually asking in a moment where the math doesn't work.

That is where standing firm matters. Am I going to trust what makes sense on paper, or am I going to trust what God has been laying on my heart? Generous people do the thing God asked them to do and trust Him with the outcome.

Where has God been nudging you to be generous, and have you been planning for it or waiting for it to somehow just happen?
