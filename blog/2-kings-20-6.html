I read the entire Bible in January. Now I'm revisiting my highlights and blogging about why each verse stood out.

2 Kings 20:6

June 5, 2026
"I will add fifteen years to your life, and I will rescue you and this city from the king of Assyria. I will defend this city for my own honor and for the sake of my servant David."2 Kings 20:6 (NLT)

It is always interesting to me when I read a verse where God does something miraculous because someone called out to Him.

Because the truth is, a lot of people call out to the Lord and things do not change the way they did here.

Hezekiah is told he is going to die. Isaiah delivers the message and walks out. And Hezekiah turns his face to the wall and weeps and prays.

Before Isaiah can even leave the courtyard, God sends him back.

Go tell him. I have heard his prayer. I have seen his tears. I am going to add fifteen years to his life.

That alone is enough to sit with.

But God does not stop there. And this is the part that stood out to me.

He does not just say because you wept.

He says I will rescue you and this city from the king of Assyria. I will defend this city for my own honor and for the sake of my servant David.

So yes, the tears mattered.

But the rescue was also for God's own honor. And for David's honor, even though David had been dead for years.

That is something I keep turning over.

Sometimes God changes the course of things because of our prayers. Sometimes He moves because of someone else who walked with Him long before us. And sometimes He acts simply for His own honor, because His name is on the line and He is not going to let it be dragged down.

It is humbling.

It reminds me that I am not the center of the story.

Hezekiah's healing was real. His tears were heard. But the bigger rescue was tied to who God is and who came before. There were threads in this miracle that had nothing to do with Hezekiah at all.

I think we sometimes forget that.

We pray and we hope God moves because of us. And sometimes He does. But sometimes He moves because of His own name. Sometimes He moves because of a promise He made to someone generations ago. Sometimes He moves because He is God, and He gets to decide.

And honestly, that is a comfort.

Because if it was all riding on my tears, my prayers, my strength, I would be in trouble.

But it is not.

God moves because of who He is.

Have you ever looked back and realized God moved in your life for reasons bigger than just you?
