I read the entire Bible in January. Now I'm revisiting my highlights and blogging about why each verse stood out.

Proverbs 20:3

October 28, 2026
"Avoiding a fight is a mark of honor; only fools insist on quarreling."Proverbs 20:3 (NLT)

This is such good advice, and it goes against how the world usually sees strength. Most of us act like strength means never backing down and getting the last word, and you see that everywhere now, in comment sections and politics and group chats, and in homes and workplaces too. The loudest voice acts like it won.

But Proverbs says avoiding a fight is a mark of honor, not weakness. Walking away takes more self-control than jumping in does. Anybody can pop off and throw a punch back, but that's just reacting, and choosing peace when you can see the fight coming is the mature thing to do.

Then the verse gets pointed. Only fools insist on quarreling. Insist is a strong word. It describes someone who won't let an argument die, who has to keep poking at it and replying until they're heard.

I've been that fool. I've kept arguments going long after they should have ended, and I've almost never looked back and thought, yeah, I'm glad I fought that one out. Usually I regret how much energy I gave to something that didn't deserve it.

I want to walk away with honor instead. I don't want to be pulled into fights easily, and when I hear the bait, I want to leave it alone and save my energy for things that actually matter.

Where is God asking you to walk away from a fight this week?
