I Have a Plan for the Whole Earth

November 11, 2026
"I have a plan for the whole earth, a hand of judgment upon all the nations."Isaiah 14:26 (NLT)

This verse is a good reminder.

God has a plan for the whole earth — every nation, every era, every kingdom that has ever risen and every one that will ever fall. All of it is under His hand.

That is the part we love to talk about. God is over everything. God is sovereign. God is in control.

But the second half of the verse isn't one we love to talk about as much.

A hand of judgment upon all the nations.

We sing plenty of songs about God's love, His mercy, His faithfulness, and His grace. Every bit of it is true. But we don't sing many songs about His judgment.

We have almost skipped that part of who He is. And it is a part. A real part. Woven all through Scripture from Genesis to Revelation. God is loving and God is just. God is kind and God brings judgment. Both are true. Both are Him.

The Bible doesn't shy away from that. God's judgment on the nations shows up over and over. On Egypt. On Babylon. On Assyria. On Nineveh. On Israel and Judah themselves when they turned from Him. And it will show up again at the end. Revelation isn't shy about it either.

We have somehow made God safe in our modern culture. We have edited Him down to the parts we like. The comforting parts. The encouraging parts. The parts that fit on a coffee mug. And we have quietly ignored the parts that make us uncomfortable.

But God isn't one or the other. He is both.

If He were only love without justice, He wouldn't be trustworthy. Evil would go unanswered. Wickedness would never be dealt with. Innocent blood would cry out forever with no response. That isn't a good God. That is a passive one.

A truly good God has to deal with evil. And He does. In His timing. On His terms. According to His plan.

I think we forget how weighty that is. And I think we forget how much comfort there actually is in it. Because if God is going to bring judgment on the nations, then no wicked ruler gets away with anything forever. No injustice is truly buried. No lie stays hidden. Every hidden thing will come out in the light one day, and God will make it right.

That isn't scary if you are His.

But it is a reason to make sure you actually are.

Because loving God means embracing all of Him. Not just the parts that make us feel good. The whole picture. His love, His grace, His mercy, and also His justice, His holiness, and His judgment.

He is coming back. And He is coming as both Savior and Judge.

Are you living like both are true?

Are you living like both are true?
