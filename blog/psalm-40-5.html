Highlighted

I read the entire Bible in January. Now I'm revisiting my highlights and blogging about why each verse stood out.

Psalm 40:5

September 30, 2026
"O Lord my God, you have performed many wonders for us. Your plans for us are too numerous to list. You would not be able to recount them all."Psalm 40:5 (NLT)

I want to remember.

That is really what this verse is about for me.

David is looking back over his life and just naming the truth. God has performed so many wonders. So many. More than he could ever list. So many that if he tried to count them all, he would never finish.

I highlighted this because I think most of us have more of God's fingerprints on our life than we ever pause to name. We move on so quickly. He answers a prayer and we say thank you and get on with our day. He rescues us out of something and we exhale and go back to normal life. He shows up in a way we couldn't have imagined, and within a week we are already worrying about the next thing.

We forget.

Not because we are ungrateful. Just because we are moving. Because life keeps happening. Because the next thing is already knocking at the door.

But David stopped.

He stopped and he said God, You have done so much. I can't even list it all.

That is the posture I want.

I want to be someone who stops on a regular basis and remembers. Who counts the wonders. Who lets the list get long in my mind. Who doesn't lose the miracles under the weight of new worries.

That is why I think journaling matters so much. And conversations with people who know your story. And even blogs like this one. Because remembering out loud is different than remembering in your head. When you say it or write it or share it, it settles in you a little deeper. It becomes part of your history in a way that a fleeting thought never could.

I want my kids to know what God has done in our family. I want my future self to know. I want the people around me to hear it and be reminded that He is still the same God who moves. I want to build little markers in my life that say He was faithful here. And here. And here.

Because the next time I'm in a hard season, I want a long list to look back on.

And here is the other thing about this verse that gets me.

David also says your plans for us are too numerous to list.

Not just the wonders He has already done. The plans He still has. The ones that haven't happened yet. The ones that are still ahead.

He isn't done.

Whatever season you are in right now, God isn't out of ideas. He isn't running low on wonders. He isn't almost through with what He wanted to do in your life. He still has plans. Too many to list.

That is a good place to end.

I don't need to know all of them. I don't need to see the whole map. I just need to remember what He has already done, and trust the rest to Him.

He has been faithful. He will be faithful.

When was the last time you actually stopped to remember and name out loud the wonders God has done in your life?
