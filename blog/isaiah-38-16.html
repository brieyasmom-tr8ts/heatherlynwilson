His Discipline Is Good

November 18, 2026
"Lord, your discipline is good, for it leads to life and health. You restore my health and allow me to live!"Isaiah 38:16 (NLT)

This is the posture we should have.

We know it. Most of us who have walked with God for any length of time have heard it. His discipline isn't punishment, and it isn't Him being harsh or trying to make us miserable. It is good, and it leads to life and health. That is the whole point of it.

But we still push against it.

We know it in our heads and we resist it with our lives. When conviction shows up, we don't want to hear it. When God is trying to redirect us, we drag our feet, and when He asks us to lay something down, we grip it tighter. We act like He is the enemy when He is really disciplining us for our good.

And it costs us. Because when we refuse His discipline, we stay stuck way longer than we should. We stay in the thing He was trying to move us out of, and we delay the healing He was trying to bring. The life He wanted for us gets pushed off because we wouldn't receive the correction that would get us there.

Hezekiah gets it here. He recognizes that what God did in his life, even the hard parts, was for his good. His discipline led to life and restored his health. It wasn't something to run from. It was something to receive.

God isn't disciplining us because He wants to hurt us. He is disciplining us because He loves us and wants more for us than the version of life we would settle for on our own. He has good plans. And sometimes the road to those good plans includes correction along the way.

I want to be someone who receives His discipline instead of resenting it, who lets Him redirect me quickly instead of dragging out the process, and sees His correction as a kindness.

Where has God been trying to discipline you, and are you receiving it or resisting it?
