I read the entire Bible in January. Now I'm revisiting my highlights and blogging about why each verse stood out.

Psalm 73:28

October 12, 2026
"But as for me, how good it is to be near God! I have made the Sovereign Lord my shelter, and I will tell everyone about the wonderful things you do."Psalm 73:28 (NLT)

I love the exclamation point in this verse.

How good it is to be near God!

That isn't a quiet observation. That isn't a polite little statement. That is a burst of joy. You can almost hear the writer saying it out loud. Like he can't contain how good it feels to be close to Him.

When was the last time we talked about being near God like that?

We say a lot of things about God. He is faithful, loving, powerful. All true. But there is something different about saying how good it is to be near Him. That is personal. It is the language of someone who has actually experienced His personal presence and can't get over it.

David doesn't just say he is near God. He says he has made the Sovereign Lord his shelter. That takes it a step further. Being near is one thing. Being under is another.

Shelter is where you go when you need protection. When the storm rolls in and you are more than exhausted. Shelter is a covering when you have nowhere else safe to land.

And David chose God as his.

He didn't choose his career. He could have. He could have tried to find safety in wealth, or his family or even in just his own strength. But no, God was his shelter. The one he ran to, who covered him when everything else couldn't.

Then the verse ends with an action.

I will tell everyone about the wonderful things you do.

Being close to God and being covered by God changes what comes out of our mouths.

We can talk about what we love. If we are truly close to God, and truly sheltered by Him, we can't help but talk about Him. It leaks out. It shows up in our conversations. I find it comes up when someone asks about my week. It bubbles into my stories and not just around Christians.

Which begs the question.

When is the last time you told anyone a wonderful thing God has done?

Not in church or in a prayer request group. Not to another Christian who was expecting to hear something spiritual.

Actually anyone. A neighbor. A coworker. The person next to you at the coffee shop. Your kids in the car. A friend who isn't walking with God. Someone who didn't think they were going to hear the name of Jesus that day.

Because if we are close to Him, and if He is our shelter, and if He is doing wonderful things, then not telling anyone is a little strange when you think about it. Don’t they deserve to know about this Good God?

Something worth wondering about.

If you had to name the last wonderful thing God did in your life, could you? And who could you tell about it today?
