I read the entire Bible in January. Now I'm revisiting my highlights and blogging about why each verse stood out.

Proverbs 1:31

October 21, 2026
"Therefore, they must eat the bitter fruit of living their own way, choking on their own schemes."Proverbs 1:31 (NLT)

The context around this verse matters.

The whole chapter is Wisdom personified as a woman standing on the busy corners of the city, crying out to anyone who will listen. She has been calling, reaching out, offering correction. And people have been ignoring her over and over. Verse 31 is the natural consequence of what happens when people keep rejecting wisdom.

They eat the bitter fruit of living their own way.

They choke on their own schemes.

We are watching this play out in real time in our culture. People are living exactly the way they want. They've thrown off anything that looks like a boundary or a moral authority. Whatever feels right is the new standard. And they call it freedom.

And they are more miserable than ever.

Anxiety is at record highs. Depression is climbing. Loneliness is being called a public health crisis. Relationships are falling apart. People don't know who they are anymore. The very lifestyle that promised to set everyone free is producing a generation that can't get out of bed without medication.

That isn't a coincidence.

It is the exact fruit Proverbs described thousands of years ago. When you reject wisdom and live your own way, you eventually have to eat what you planted. Your own schemes catch up with you. The freedom you thought you were chasing becomes a cage of consequences you didn't see coming.

The saddest part is that Wisdom is still calling. She hasn't gone quiet. God hasn't stopped reaching. He's offering direction to anyone who will take it, inviting people back to a way of living that actually leads to life. But you can't keep rejecting Him and expect a different outcome than the one He has already warned about.

I don't say any of this smugly. I say it grieving.

Because I'm not watching this from a distance. I know people living this out. I've sat with families going through it. I've looked into the eyes of young people who were handed a lie — that the world had better answers than God — and they're drowning in the fruit of it.

The way back is the same as it has always been. Turn around. Listen to Wisdom. Fear the Lord. Take the correction. Choose His way over your own.

Where in your life have you been living your own way and starting to taste the bitter fruit of it?
