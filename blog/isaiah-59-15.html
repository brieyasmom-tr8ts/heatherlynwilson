Truth Is Gone

November 27, 2026
"Yes, truth is gone, and anyone who renounces evil is attacked. The Lord looked and was displeased to find there was no justice."Isaiah 59:15 (NLT)

This passage sounds exactly like our culture today.

Truth is gone. If you speak the truth in almost any public space right now, you will pay for it. Truth about biology, morality, God, the Bible, right and wrong. The moment you speak it, someone comes for you.

Anyone who renounces evil is attacked. Read that line again slowly. The people who dare to name what is actually evil and refuse to be part of it are the ones who get labeled as the problem. Not the people doing the evil. The ones speaking against it.

That is exactly where we are.

Christians who hold to biblical truth get called hateful, and parents who protect their kids from what culture wants to push on them get called extremists. Anyone who dares to say a hard word about the direction things are going gets piled on. The moral order has been flipped completely upside down. The wicked are celebrated and the ones renouncing evil are attacked.

And Isaiah is describing this thousands of years ago.

Which means this isn't new. It is the same pattern that has played out in every generation when a culture drifts far enough from God. Truth becomes offensive and righteousness becomes intolerable, and anyone who stands for what is right gets treated like the enemy.

The Lord looked and was displeased to find there was no justice.

He sees it. He isn't blind to what is happening, and He isn't indifferent. That should tell us a lot about how He views the state of our culture. This displeases Him, and He isn't shrugging it off. The injustice, the deception, and the persecution of those who try to walk in truth grieve and anger Him.

But the verses right after this one are the ones we need to hold onto.

Because Isaiah goes on to say that when God saw no one stepping in to help, He stepped in Himself. He put on righteousness as body armor and the helmet of salvation, and He moved on behalf of His people.

Many read that as pointing straight to Jesus.

When no human being could fix what was broken, God Himself put on flesh and stepped into the mess. He did what no one else could do. He is still the one who sets things right, even now.

So when the world feels like Isaiah 59, and it does, we don't have to spiral. We aren't the first generation to watch truth disappear. And God isn't sitting still. He sees it and He is at work, and in the end He is the one who will make everything right.

Our job is to keep standing. That means speaking truth and renouncing evil even when it costs us, and walking with Him even when the culture pulls hard in every other direction.

Because He sees. And He isn't done.

Are you willing to stand for truth even when it costs you, and are you trusting that God is still at work even when the world looks like Isaiah 59?
