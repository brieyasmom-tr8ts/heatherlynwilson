I read the entire Bible in January. Now I'm revisiting my highlights and blogging about why each verse stood out.

Not Clapbacks or Receipts

September 25, 2026
"See how many enemies I have and how viciously they hate me! Protect me! Rescue my life from them! Do not let me be disgraced, for in you I take refuge. May integrity and honesty protect me, for I put my hope in you."Psalm 25:19-21 (NLT)

I wonder if this was a hard social media day when I highlighted these verses.

Being the Co-CEO of GiveSendGo, there are days when the hate online is loud. Really loud. Vicious in the way David describes here. Coming from every direction. Saying things that aren't true. Attacking my character, the mission, me personally.

Days when it feels like everyone hates me.

They can be brutal.

And I wonder if that is why I highlighted these verses. Because David felt it too. Not the online part. But the vicious hatred part. He knew what it was like to look around and feel outnumbered by people who wanted him gone.

But watch how quickly he shifts.

He names the reality. See how many enemies I have. He doesn't pretend it isn't there or put on a brave face and act like it doesn't sting.

Then he goes straight to God.

Protect me. Rescue my life from them. Do not let me be disgraced.

He doesn't fight back or go on the counter attack. He turns his face toward God and asks Him to handle it.

And then he says something I think is beautiful.

May integrity and honesty protect me.

Not clapbacks. Not receipts. Integrity and honesty.

He is saying God, if I just keep walking with You, if I just keep doing what is right, that itself will be my protection. Because a life of integrity doesn't need to defend itself the way a life without it does. The truth stands on its own over time, and character speaks louder than accusations. God is the one who vindicates.

That is such a good reminder for anyone who has ever felt piled on.

You don't have to answer every voice or correct every lie. You just have to keep walking with God and keep letting integrity and honesty be your posture.

And let Him handle the rest.

I want the hard days to actually mean something.

Because if I let it, criticism can grow me. Hard seasons can carve away the parts of me that weren't really from God to begin with.

That doesn't mean I have to enjoy the hate. It just means I don't have to waste it.

And no matter what anyone says about me on any given day, God is still working. He is still shaping me and finishing what He started in me. And the noise of the internet doesn't get to interrupt that.

I don't want to be distracted. Not by the loudest voices. Not by how many people dislike me on a given day.

For in you I take refuge.

That is who I'm running to when the arrows are flying.

What are you trying to defend right now that you could let your integrity defend instead?
