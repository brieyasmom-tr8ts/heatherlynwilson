Write the Notes

August 19, 2026

I was in the shower getting ready to leave on a cruise with my mom the next day when I felt like the Lord was telling me to do something.

Write Dwight a note for every day I was gone.

It wasn’t a huge thing. It wasn’t complicated. But almost immediately, I started coming up with reasons why I didn’t need to do it.

Well, if I write notes to Dwight, I should probably write them to Kenzie too.

I’m leaving tomorrow.

When am I going to have time to write all these notes?

Maybe I can do something else instead.

I had plenty of reasons.

But I knew what God was asking me to do. He was asking me to make an extra effort to pour into Dwight during the time I was away.

And I didn’t really want to do it.

So eventually it came down to a pretty simple choice.

Do it or don’t.

Obey or don’t.

Sometimes knowing isn’t the problem

I’ve been thinking about this a lot lately.

As Christians, we know a lot of things we’re supposed to do.

Love people.

Forgive.

Be generous.

Encourage each other.

Tell the truth.

Pray.

Trust God.

Most of those things probably aren’t new information to you.

But then there are times when one of those general truths becomes really specific.

It’s not just, “Christians should encourage people.”

It’s, “Heather, I want you to encourage Dwight.”

Now I have something to respond to.

I think those moments are important because it’s really easy to keep our faith in the general.

Yes, I believe we should love people.

Yes, I believe we should be generous.

Yes, I believe we should forgive.

But who am I supposed to love when they’re difficult to love?

What am I supposed to give?

Who do I need to forgive?

Who needs encouragement from me today?

That’s different.

We can keep learning instead of responding

I love reading the Bible. I love Bible studies. I love learning something I didn’t know before.

But I’m realizing more and more that I need to pay just as much attention to what I’m doing with what God is showing me.

James 1:22 says:

“Do not merely listen to the word, and so deceive yourselves. Do what it says.”James 1:22

That’s pretty straightforward.

There’s a time to study.

There’s a time to ask questions.

There’s a time to pray for wisdom.

But there’s also a time when we already know enough to take the next step.

I didn’t need to pray about whether encouraging Dwight was biblical.

I didn’t need to look up verses about encouraging our children.

I didn’t need another Bible study.

I needed to write the notes.

What’s your one thing?

I did write the notes.

And it made me think about how often we ask God to show us something new when He may already be waiting for us to respond to something He’s shown us.

So today, ask Him:

“Is there something You’ve already shown me that I haven’t responded to?”

If something comes to mind, don’t overcomplicate it.

Just take the next step.

Know the truth. Go live it.
