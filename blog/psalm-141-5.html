I read the entire Bible in January. Now I'm revisiting my highlights and blogging about why each verse stood out.

Psalm 141:5

October 19, 2026
"Let the godly strike me! It will be a kindness! If they correct me, it is soothing medicine. Don't let me refuse it. But I pray constantly against the wicked and their deeds."Psalm 141:5 (NLT)

How often do we actually want the godly to correct us?

Not many of us would raise our hand for that. We say we want to grow, and be sharpened. We would tell you we want to be more like Jesus. But when someone actually walks up and tells us something hard about ourselves, most of us get defensive. We push back and find a reason why they are wrong or misinformed or overstepping.

David does the opposite here. He doesn't just tolerate correction from the godly. He invites it.

Let the godly strike me! It will be a kindness!

That is a wild sentence. He calls it a kindness. A soothing medicine. Something he doesn't want to refuse. He is essentially saying that being corrected by someone who loves God is one of the best things that can happen to him.

Because he understands what correction from a godly person actually is. It isn't an attack or an attempt to make him feel small. It is someone who loves God enough to tell him the truth. And truth, even when it stings, is one of the most valuable gifts anyone can give us.

We should be inviting this into our lives, each one of us should have people close enough and trusted enough that when they see something off in us, they can say so. And more importantly, we should be the kind of people who receive it instead of resist it.

That means picking those people carefully. Not everyone who wants to correct you is qualified to. The verse is specific. It's the godly. The ones who are walking with God themselves. Those whose lives show they are pursuing Him. Their correction is different than criticism from someone who just doesn't like you or wants to tear you down.

Godly correction is soothing medicine. Everything else is just noise.

Who in your life has permission to correct you, and are you the kind of person who receives it as kindness instead of pushing it away?
